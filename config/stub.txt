loop gateCount read gateX:int gateY:int gateZ:int nx:float ny:float nz:float radius:int
read cellCount:int
loop cellCount read cellX:int cellY:int cellZ:int cellType:word(6)

gameloop
read remainingTime:int
loop playerCount read x:float y:float z:float vx:float vy:float vz:float fx:float fy:float fz:float ux:float uy:float uz:float nextGate:int lap:int boosts:int boostTimer:int battery:int cutout:int state:int shield:int ghost:int item:int wx:float wy:float wz:float
loop cellCount read respawnTimer:int
write ANGLE 70 0 30 0

STATEMENT
Fly your drone through the gates, in order and in the right direction.

INPUT
playerCount: number of drones (2 to 4)
myId: your drone id
laps: laps to finish
gateCount: number of gates, gate 0 is the start and finish line
gateX: centre of the gate
nx: direction to fly through the gate (unit vector)
radius: radius of the ring
cellCount: number of power cells
cellType: CHARGE, SHIELD or GHOST
pylonCount: number of pylons (vertical columns from floor to ceiling)
pylonX: position of the pylon axis
pylonRadius: radius of the pylon
x: position of the drone
vx: speed of the drone
fx: forward unit vector of the drone
ux: up unit vector of the drone
nextGate: id of the next gate to clear
lap: laps already completed
boosts: boosts available
boostTimer: turns until the next boost is recharged
battery: turns left before the battery is empty
cutout: turns left with the motors cut
state: 0 destroyed, 1 flying, 2 finished
shield: turns left with a shield
ghost: turns left as a ghost
item: held item, 0 none, 1 SHIELD, 2 GHOST
wx: current rotation speed: the rotation made last turn (rotation vector, radians)
respawnTimer: turns before the cell comes back, 0 if it is there
remainingTime: milliseconds left in your time bank for the whole race

OUTPUT
[ANGLE|ACRO] THROTTLE YAW PITCH ROLL [BOOST] [USE] [CAM angle] [message]
